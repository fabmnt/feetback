# Feetback
Feetback helps customers get detailed and categoryzed feedback from their users.
Feetback stands out for its easy and minimal set up, to quickly start collecting valuable, categorized and detailed feedback from their users.

Feetback's client script is easy to set up and must not interfere at all with customer's app business logic. It's a subtle floating button that can be used by their users whenever they have a problem. The whole experience of sending feedback items should be customizable for every Customer App.

Feetback's Dashboard goal is to show all the feedback sent by the customer's users in an readable way. It must be easy to see important feedback first.
